Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

CINOVA ("we", "our", "the app") is a movie and video streaming app operated by Cinova Productions. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password when you sign up. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and stored in hashed form. We cannot see your password.
  • Usage information: the movies you add to My List, and your watch progress (which movie, how far you watched, and when), so we can offer "Continue Watching".
  • Technical information: basic data such as IP address, device type and app version, which our service providers may log automatically.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location or microphone data.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and log you in
  • To save your My List and watch progress
  • To keep the app secure and fix bugs
  • To contact you about important service or account matters

3. Service providers

  • Supabase for account login and for storing your My List and watch history
  • Bunny.net for delivering video content (it may log IP addresses to deliver video)

These providers process data only to provide their services to us and have their own privacy policies.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers above, or if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all related data (email, My List, watch history) by emailing cinova785@gmail.com. We will process the request within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We use industry-standard services and access rules so that each user can only access their own personal data. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

CINOVA is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
